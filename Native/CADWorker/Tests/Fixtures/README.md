# Synthetic CAD fixtures

`box.step` and `box.iges` are independent analytic 10 × 20 × 30 mm boxes,
generated by the repository's `FixtureGenerator.cpp` with the pinned OCCT build.
The geometry is authored for this repository and redistributable with it; no
private or third-party model was copied. Header authors/organizations/names and
timestamps are fixed synthetic values. STEP uses AP214; IGES uses face mode.

The development generator additionally creates translated boxes, inch-unit STEP,
cylinders, cones, spheres and a 10 × 10 mm planar face with a 4 × 4 mm square hole
in a temporary test directory. These test geometry bounds, units, transforms,
outward normals, collapsed poles and trim area independently of importer output.
`CADFixtureGenerator` is never included in product bundles.
