This product makes use of facilities provided by Open CASCADE Technology
(OCCT), copyright OPEN CASCADE SAS and its contributors. It uses OCCT 7.9.3,
official commit a016080bf6738d6aeae020badee4e888ad1540a5, in one place: CADWorker,
the separate helper executable that imports STEP and IGES files and links the
library statically. The app and its Quick Look extensions do not link OCCT.
